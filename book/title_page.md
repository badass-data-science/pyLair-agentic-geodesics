# Agentic Geodesic Lair Design for Supervillains
### Computational Geometry and Agentic AI with pyLair and pyFit

*as told by our heroine*

---

This book is the long-form companion to two shorter, funnier documents: [`blog-posts/introducing-pylair.md`](../blog-posts/introducing-pylair.md) ("Introducing pyLair, or, How Our Heroine Designed Her Geodesic Secret Lair") and pyFit's own `blog-posts/introducing-pyfit.md`, in the `pyFit-agentic-polygon-nesting` repository. Reading either first is encouraged, not required — this book stands on its own, and returns the favor by explaining, at far greater length and with far more pictures, exactly how the dome those posts describe actually got designed, accounted for, exported, and finally cut from real material.
