# About the "for Supervillains" Book Series

Ordinary textbooks assume you just want to learn the material — the *for Supervillains* series assumes you also want to conquer the world! This volume *is* the *Geodesic Lair Design for Supervillains* that this series' own companion volume, *Agentic Time Series Forecasting for Supervillains*, already promised was coming, several NDAs later than planned.

Same heroine, same Ultimate Cunning Master Plan&trade;, same standing offer of mojitos, poolside, while the computer does the arithmetic. No prior volume required — this book explains everything it needs from scratch — but readers of the other one will notice a familiar voice, and a shared conviction that rigor and villainy are not, in fact, mutually exclusive.
