# About the "for Supervillains" Book Series

Ordinary textbooks assume you just want to learn the material — the *...for Supervillains* series assumes you also want to conquer the world! This volume is the most recent in the series, after *Agentic Time Series Forecasting for Supervillains* and *Systems Thinking for Supervillains*.

Same Heroine, same Ultimate Cunning Master Plan&trade;, same standing offer of mojitos, poolside, while the computer does the arithmetic. No prior volume required — this book explains everything it needs from scratch — but readers of the others will notice a familiar voice, and a shared conviction that rigor and villainy are not, in fact, mutually exclusive.
